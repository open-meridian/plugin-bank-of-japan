# Pages

Both pages are the data role's, under **Manage**, for the plugin's admins.
Neither shows a rate or a price. Each is also a read tool on the
deployment's MCP, answering what the page shows.

## Connection (`read_connection`)

The head's status; **Key** (the mode, "No key needed"); **Limits and use**
(one request at a time, at most one every five seconds, each database
asked every hour, a day read only when wanted; the requests made, the last
check and error); **Stream** (it does not stream); and **Sources**, a tab
of its own: FM01, FM02, FM08 and IR01, each with its state, latest day and
calls, and in its details where it reads and that nothing of it is kept.

## Datasets (`read_datasets`)

**Served**: its two datasets as declared, whether each is licensed, what it
recorded and declined, and the credit the Bank's API notice asks for;
**Wants**, a tab of its own: the wants it answered, and whether it may read
an instrument's record; then **Records**, a tab of its own: each record and
currency, what it is recorded as, its convention, and your deployment's
instrument for it or why there is none, and each series read and not
carried, searched and paged to fit one screen.
